Keep Them Fresh

Simple Lash Aftercare Tips

A few gentle daily habits help your lashes stay fluffy, clean, and long-lasting.

1

Keep lashes dry for 24 hours

2

No oil-based products near the eyes

3

Do not pull or pick

4

Brush gently with a clean spoolie

5

Sleep on your back if you can

6

Book refills every 2 to 3 weeks
